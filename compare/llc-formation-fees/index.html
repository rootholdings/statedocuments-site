LLC filing fees by state, ranked: all 50 states and DC

The state filing fee to form a standard domestic LLC, from each state's official business filing office, ranked from lowest to highest by the first dollar amount listed. Some states add publication or other requirements; open a state for details.

As of November 15, 2026. We list a figure only when we confirmed it on an official state page that day: 46 of 51. "Check with state" means we could not confirm it, so we do not guess; the link goes to the official office. Fees change; the official page is always the authority. Ranks use the first dollar amount listed (usually the online fee where two are shown).
