Certificate of good standing fees: all 50 states and DC

What each state business filing office charges for a certificate of good standing (sometimes called a certificate of existence or status).

As of November 15, 2026. We list a figure only when we confirmed it on an official state page that day: 32 of 51. "Check with state" means we could not confirm it, so we do not guess; the link goes to the official office. Fees change; the official page is always the authority. Sorting by fee uses the first dollar amount listed.

AlaskaCheck with statewww.commerce.alaska.gov (official office)

FloridaCertificate of Status: $5.dos.fl.gov
GeorgiaCheck with statesos.ga.gov (official office)
HawaiiGood Standing Certificate (Certificate of Compliance): $5 (optional expedited review adds $10).cca.hawaii.gov
IdahoCertificate of Existence: $10.sos.idaho.gov

MinnesotaCertificate of Good Standing: $5 per certificate (per the copy and certificate request form).www.sos.mn.gov
MississippiCheck with statewww.sos.ms.gov (official office)
MissouriCertificate of Good Standing: $10.www.sos.mo.gov

NevadaCheck with statewww.nvsos.gov (official office)

New MexicoCheck with statewww.sos.nm.gov (official office)

North CarolinaCertificate of Existence: $15.www.sosnc.gov
