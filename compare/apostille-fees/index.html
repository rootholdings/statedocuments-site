Apostille fees and processing times: all 50 states and DC

One sortable table of what each state apostille office charges and how long it takes. Fees confirmed for 47 of 51; processing times for 36 of 51. Sorting by fee uses the first dollar amount listed.

As of November 15, 2026. We list a figure only when we confirmed it on an official state page that day: 47 of 51. "Check with state" means we could not confirm it, so we do not guess; the link goes to the official office. Fees change; the official page is always the authority. Many states charge per document, and some charge more for expedited or walk-in service.

Alabama$5 per document.Check with stateAlabama Secretary of Statewww.sos.alabama.gov
Alaska$5 per certificate.The office says it makes every effort to turn an order around the same day it is received.Office of the Lieutenant Governor of Alaskaltgov.alaska.gov
Arizona$3 per document. Walk-in service carries a $25 expedite fee, with a limit of 6 documents.By mail: 10–20 business days. Walk-in: same visit with a $25 expedite fee (limit of 6 documents).Arizona Secretary of Stateazsos.gov
Arkansas$10.00 per document.Check with stateArkansas Secretary of Statewww.sos.arkansas.gov
California$20 per apostille. The page also lists a $6 special handling fee per different official signature in some cases.Mail requests are handled in the order received, and the office cannot expedite mail. Same-day service is available in person.California Secretary of Statewww.sos.ca.gov
Colorado$5 per document routine; $15 per document expedited.Check with stateColorado Secretary of Statewww.coloradosos.gov
Connecticut$40 per document ($15 for child adoption). Expedited service adds $50 per document.Regular orders: 5–7 business days after the unit receives the documents. e-Apostille and expedited orders: within about 24 hours / one business day.Connecticut Secretary of the Stateportal.ct.gov · portal.ct.gov
Delaware$30.00 for any documents that are presented simultaneously (non-commercial); $30.00 per document (commercial). Expedited service is an additional fee for commercial or non-personal documents.Mail requests are typically processed on the same business day. In-person requests for documents intended for personal use are typically processed on the same business day.Delaware Division of Corporationscorp.delaware.gov
District of Columbia$15 per document authenticated. The cost is the same at the Mail In Service Center and the Walk In Service Center.Walk-in: same day, usually 30 minutes or less. Mail: returned in about five business days or less.DC Office of Notary Commissions and Authenticationsos.dc.gov · os.dc.gov
Florida$10 per document, or $20 for documents certified by a Florida county Clerk of Court.At least five (5) working/business days. Processing time varies with volume, requests are completed in date order, and expedited service is not offered.Florida Department of State, Division of Corporationsdos.fl.gov · notaries.dos.fl.gov
Georgia$3 per document.Normally one to two business days; documents brought to the office usually take under twenty minutes, depending on volume.Georgia Superior Court Clerks' Cooperative Authority (GSCCCA)www.gsccca.org
Hawaii$3 per document. A fee change for 2027 has been reported, so check the official page.About 7–10 business days. No expedited service.Office of the Lieutenant Governor of Hawaiiltgov.hawaii.gov
Idaho$10 per document.Requests are processed daily as received. If you are mailing a request on a deadline, allow for mailing and processing time.Idaho Secretary of Statesos.idaho.gov
Illinois$2 per apostille or certificate. Each document needs its own.Mail requests: 7–14 business days.Illinois Secretary of State, Index Departmentwww.ilsos.gov
Indiana$2 per authentication. The page lists no fee for birth and death certificates, adoption documents, and student transcripts and diplomas.Normally 1–2 business days from receipt. Requests over 30 documents may take longer.Indiana Secretary of State, Business Services Divisionwww.in.gov
IowaCheck with stateWalk-in: same day. Mail and upload requests are handled in date order; no turnaround is published.Iowa Secretary of Statesos.iowa.gov
Kansas$10 per document (raised on March 2, 2026).Generally processed within 3-5 business days.Kansas Secretary of Statewww.sos.ks.gov · www.sos.ks.gov
Kentucky$5 per document.Mail: completed within 3 business days, returned by first-class mail. Walk-in: done while you wait.Kentucky Secretary of Statewww.sos.ky.gov
Louisiana$20 per document; $10 if the document relates to an adoption.Allow two to three business days for processing, per the office's authentication request form.Louisiana Secretary of Statewww.sos.la.gov · www.sos.la.gov
Maine$10 per certification.10–15 business days.Maine Secretary of Statewww.maine.gov
Maryland$5 per certification or apostille.Regular mail: 7–14 business days. USPS Priority Mail: about 3–4 days.Maryland Secretary of Statesos.maryland.gov
Massachusetts$6 per document.Mail: two to three weeks. Counter: up to 3 documents while you wait; 4 or more ready after 3 pm the next business day.Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth, Commissions Sectionwww.sec.state.ma.us
Michigan$1 per document.Mail requests: turnaround of 1–2 weeks, per the Office of the Great Seal.Michigan Department of State, Office of the Great Sealwww.michigan.gov
MinnesotaCheck with stateCheck with stateMinnesota Secretary of Statewww.sos.mn.gov
Mississippi$5 per document.Check with stateMississippi Secretary of Statewww.sos.ms.gov
Missouri$10 per certification or authentication.Check with stateMissouri Secretary of Statewww.sos.mo.gov
Montana$10 per document or notarization.Normal processing time is 3–5 business days from receipt by the Notary and Certifications Division.Montana Secretary of Statesosmt.gov
Nebraska$10 per document or notarization.Normally 3–5 business days from receipt. Walk-ins are completed the same day.Nebraska Secretary of Statesos.nebraska.gov
Nevada$20 per apostille or certification. Expedited service is available for an additional fee.The official apostille page lists a standard processing time of 2 weeks. The official fees page says regular processing takes approximately 4–6 weeks. Check both official pages for the current estimate.Nevada Secretary of Statewww.nvsos.gov · www.nvsos.gov
New Hampshire$10 per document. Same-day service for 10 or more documents adds $25 per 10 documents.In person: usually same day; large batches may be ready the next business day.New Hampshire Secretary of Statewww.sos.nh.gov
New Jersey$25 per certificate; $5 when obtained for the adoption of a child.Regular processing: 12-20 business days. Expedited processing: 8.5 business hours from receipt and entry (also described as next business day). In-person delivery: up to 2.5 hours, subject to daily workload.New Jersey Division of Revenue and Enterprise Serviceswww.nj.gov · www.njportal.com
New Mexico$3 per document.Check with stateNew Mexico Secretary of Statewww.sos.nm.gov
New York$10 per document.Check with stateNew York Department of Statedos.ny.gov
North Carolina$10 per document.Check with stateNorth Carolina Secretary of Statewww.sosnc.gov
North Dakota$10 per authentication, plus a $5 record search fee for each different notary.Usually within 3 business days if everything is in order; returned by first-class mail.North Dakota Secretary of Statewww.sos.nd.gov
OhioCheck with stateCheck with stateOhio Secretary of Statewww.ohiosos.gov
Oklahoma$25 per apostille ($10 for international adoption proceedings); $20 per authentication.Walk-in: about 15 minutes per document, while you wait.Oklahoma Secretary of Stateoklahoma.gov
Oregon$10 per apostille.Check with stateOregon Secretary of State, Corporation Divisionsos.oregon.gov
Pennsylvania$15 per document.Mail: 5–7 business days after receipt, plus mailing time. Walk-in: while you wait.Pennsylvania Department of Statewww.pa.gov
Rhode Island$5 per document.3-5 business days.Rhode Island Secretary of Statewww.sos.ri.gov
South Carolina$5 per document.Check with stateSouth Carolina Secretary of Statesos.sc.gov
South Dakota$25 per document; an optional $50 expedite fee is listed.Mailed requests: within 2 business days of receipt; most orders take 1–2 business days.South Dakota Secretary of Statesdsos.gov
Tennessee$2 per apostille or authentication.Check with stateTennessee Secretary of Statesos-prod.tnsosgovfiles.com
Texas$15 per document (not per page); $10 for adoption proceedings, capped at $100 per child.In person: same day (up to 10 documents). Drop box: processed within 72 hours. Mail: first come, first served.Texas Secretary of Statewww.sos.texas.gov · www.sos.state.tx.us
UtahCheck with stateIn-person: within 10 minutes (standard) to 2 hours of your appointment time (appointments are required; walk-ins are not accepted). Mail-in: typically 1-3 business days once documents are received, not including mailing transit time. Documents are not processed on weekends or holidays.Utah Office of the Lieutenant Governorauthentications.utah.gov · authentications.utah.gov
Vermont$10 per apostille or authentication.Check with stateVermont Secretary of Statesos.vermont.gov
Virginia$10 per document; $5 for each additional document signed by the same official on the same date for the same country.Mail: generally 7–10 business days from when you send the documents. Drop box: returned within 5–7 business days. Appointments: most requests processed the same day. A seasonal notice on the main page has listed about 4 weeks.Secretary of the Commonwealth of Virginiawww.commonwealth.virginia.gov
Washington$15 per document; expedited processing adds $100 per 10 documents.Standard: about 7–10 business days. Expedited: about 2–3 business days. Same-day in person for an extra fee.Washington Secretary of Statewww.sos.wa.gov
West Virginia$10 per certification; additional documents signed by the same official are $5 each.The office aims to process requests within 48 business hours; walk-in customers are usually served within minutes. Times can run longer.West Virginia Secretary of Statesos.wv.gov
Wisconsin$10 per document standard; $35 per document expedited.Standard: about 7–20 days from receipt. Expedited: 2–4 business days.Wisconsin Secretary of Statesos.wi.gov
Wyoming$20 per apostille or authentication.Check with stateWyoming Secretary of Statesos.wyo.gov
