LLC annual report fees and due dates: all 50 states and DC

What each state charges an LLC for its annual or periodic report, and when it is due, where we could confirm it on an official page.

As of November 13, 2026. We list a figure only when we confirmed it on an official state page that day: 34 of 51. "Check with state" means we could not confirm it, so we do not guess; the link goes to the official office. Fees change; the official page is always the authority. Report fees confirmed for 34 of 51 and due dates for 25 of 51. Sorting by fee uses the first dollar amount listed.

AlabamaCheck with stateCheck with statewww.sos.alabama.gov (official office)
AlaskaCheck with stateCheck with statewww.commerce.alaska.gov (official office)
ArizonaCheck with stateCheck with stateazcc.gov (official office)
ArkansasCheck with stateCheck with statewww.sos.arkansas.gov (official office)
CaliforniaStatement of Information: $20, due within 90 days of forming and every two years after.Due within 90 days of forming, then every two years.www.sos.ca.gov
ColoradoPeriodic report (online): $25; late filing penalty $50.Filed in the LLC's periodic report month (shown on its Summary page); it can be filed from two months before to two months after that month without penalty.www.coloradosos.gov · www.coloradosos.gov (due date)
ConnecticutAnnual report: $80.Filed online each year between January 1 and March 31.business.ct.gov
DelawareDelaware LLCs do not file an annual report. They pay a $400 annual tax by June 1; paying late adds a $200 penalty plus 1.5% monthly interest.Annual tax due by June 1.corp.delaware.gov
District of ColumbiaBiennial report for-profit entities: $300. Filing after April 1 of the year it is due adds a $100 late fee.Biennial report; filing after April 1 of the year it is due adds a $100 late fee.dlcp.dc.gov
FloridaAnnual report: $138.75; $538.75 if received after May 1.Annual report must be received by May 1 to avoid the higher fee.dos.fl.gov
GeorgiaCheck with stateAnnual registration is filed each year between January 1 and April 1.georgia.gov
HawaiiAnnual report: $15 (optional expedited review adds $25).Check with statecca.hawaii.gov
IdahoCheck with stateCheck with statesos.idaho.gov (official office)
IllinoisAnnual report (Form LLC-50.1): $75 filing fee; a $100 late penalty applies after the due date.Check with statewww.ilsos.gov
IndianaCheck with stateCheck with statewww.in.gov (official office)
IowaBiennial report: $30 online or $45 on paper.Biennial report due by April 1 in odd-numbered years (filing opens January 1).sos.iowa.gov
KansasInformation report: $90 online or $110 on paper.Information reports are filed every two years: by April 15 of each even year for LLCs formed in an even year, or each odd year for LLCs formed in an odd year.sos.ks.gov · www.sos.ks.gov
KentuckyAnnual report: $15.Due by June 30 each year (can be filed any time from January 1).www.sos.ky.gov · www.sos.ky.gov (due date)
LouisianaAnnual report: $35.Check with statewww.sos.la.gov
MaineAnnual report (domestic LLC): $85.The legal filing deadline is June 1 each year.www.maine.gov · www.maine.gov (due date)
MarylandCheck with stateCheck with statedat.maryland.gov (official office)
MassachusettsAnnual report: $500.Check with statewww.sec.state.ma.us
MichiganAnnual statement: $25 for an LLC ($75 for a professional LLC); a $50 late fee applies if received after February 15.Due by February 15 of the year it is due; a $50 late fee applies after that.www.michigan.gov
MinnesotaCheck with stateCheck with statewww.sos.mn.gov (official office)
MississippiAnnual report: $0 (no fee) for a Mississippi LLC.Can be filed on or after January 1 and is due by April 15 each year; filed online only.www.sos.ms.gov · www.sos.ms.gov (due date)
MissouriCheck with stateCheck with statewww.sos.mo.gov (official office)
MontanaAnnual report: $20 if filed before April 15; $35 after April 15 (fee schedule effective July 1, 2022).The fee schedule prices the report at $20 before April 15 and $35 after, so April 15 is the date that matters.sosmt.gov
NebraskaCheck with stateBiennial report due by April 1 in odd-numbered years; delinquent after June 16.sos.nebraska.gov
NevadaCheck with stateCheck with statewww.nvsos.gov (official office)
New HampshireAnnual report: $100, plus a $50 late fee if late.Check with statewww.sos.nh.gov
New JerseyAnnual report (LLC): $75.Check with statewww.nj.gov
New MexicoCheck with stateCheck with statewww.sos.nm.gov (official office)
New YorkBiennial Statement: $9, due every two years in the month the LLC was formed.Biennial Statement due every two years, in the month the LLC was formed.dos.ny.gov
North CarolinaAnnual report: $200 on paper or $203 online.Due April 15 each year, starting the year after the LLC is formed.www.sosnc.gov
North DakotaAnnual report: $50.Filing deadline: November 15 (business LLC).www.sos.nd.gov
OhioCheck with stateCheck with statewww.ohiosos.gov (official office)
OklahomaAnnual certificate (Oklahoma or foreign LLC): $25.Annual certificate due each year on the LLC's anniversary date ($25).oklahoma.gov · oklahoma.gov (due date)
OregonAnnual renewal (domestic LLC): $100.Check with statesos.oregon.gov
PennsylvaniaAnnual report for-profit entities: $7.Due by September 30 each year for LLCs, starting the year after formation.www.pa.gov · www.pa.gov (due date)
Rhode IslandAnnual report: $50 (separately, the state lists a $400 minimum annual tax paid to the Division of Taxation).Check with statewww.sos.ri.gov
South CarolinaCheck with stateCheck with statesos.sc.gov (official office)
South DakotaAnnual report: $55 online or $70 on paper.Check with statesdsos.gov
TennesseeCheck with stateCheck with statesos.tn.gov (official office)
TexasCheck with stateCheck with statewww.sos.texas.gov (official office)
UtahAnnual renewal (domestic LLC): $18, which includes a $5 portal surcharge; late renewal adds $10 (Fiscal Year 2026 fee schedule).Check with statecommerce.utah.gov
VermontAnnual report: $45.Check with statesos.vermont.gov
VirginiaAnnual registration fee: $50, due by the last day of the month the LLC was formed. Online payment has no processing fee.Due by the last day of the month the LLC was formed.www.scc.virginia.gov
WashingtonAnnual report: $70 ($95 with delinquency fee). The initial report is $10.Due by the last day of the month the LLC was first formed; can be filed up to 180 days early. A $25 delinquency fee applies after that date.www.sos.wa.gov · www.sos.wa.gov (due date)
West VirginiaAnnual report: $25 if received by June 30; $75 after June 30 (includes a $50 late fee). Online filings add $1.Due by June 30 each year for LLCs formed on or before December 31 of the previous year.sos.wv.gov
WisconsinAnnual report: $40 on paper (a lower online fee is listed).Due by the end of the calendar quarter in which the LLC's registration anniversary falls (March 31, June 30, September 30 or December 31), starting the year after it registers.dfi.wi.gov · dfi.wi.gov (due date)
WyomingAnnual report license tax: $60 or two-tenths of one mill on the dollar ($.0002) of the company's Wyoming assets, whichever is greater.Due on or before the first day of the month the LLC was first filed, starting the year after formation (example from the state: filed February 16, 2025, so due February 1 each year from 2026).sos.wyo.gov · sos.wyo.gov (due date)
