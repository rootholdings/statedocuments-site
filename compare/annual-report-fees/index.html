LLC annual report fees and due dates: all 50 states and DC

What each state charges an LLC for its annual or periodic report, and when it is due, where we could confirm it on an official page.

As of November 13, 2026. We list a figure only when we confirmed it on an official state page that day: 16 of 51. "Check the official page" means we could not confirm it, so we do not guess. Fees change; the official page is always the authority. Sorting by fee uses the first dollar amount listed. Due dates are shown only where the official source stated one.
