Business filings: where to form, report, and prove good standing

Every state has one office that registers businesses. It's usually the Secretary of State. That office handles forming an LLC or corporation, annual or biennial reports, and certificates of good standing. You file with them directly, and the fee goes to the state.

Watch for look-alike mail. Several state offices warn about private companies that send official-looking letters asking for money to file annual reports or buy certificates. Check any request against your state's own page before paying.
