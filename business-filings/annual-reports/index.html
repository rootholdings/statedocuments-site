Annual and periodic business reports

Most states require LLCs and corporations to file a regular report (annual or every two years) that confirms basic details like the registered agent and principal address. Some states also charge a fee or franchise tax with it.

Why it matters

Missing a report can put a business out of good standing and, eventually, lead the state to dissolve or revoke it. Reinstatement is usually possible but costs more.

How to file

File directly with your state's business filing office. Due dates and fees differ by state and entity type, so use the official page in the directory.

Some state offices warn about private companies sending official-looking notices that charge extra to "file" a report. Your state's own page is the place to file.
