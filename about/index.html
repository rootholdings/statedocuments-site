About this guide, sources, and disclaimer

What we are

StateDocuments.com is a privately run, independent information site. It explains how common state documents work and links to the government offices that handle them. It is not run by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any government agency.

What we do not do

  • We do not issue, certify, apostille, file, order, or sell any document.
  • We do not prepare forms or act on anyone's behalf.
  • We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, ID numbers, or payment details. There are no forms on this site.
  • We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. For advice about your situation, talk to a licensed attorney or the office itself.

How we check facts

For every state, we start from the official government page and link to it first. Links and fees on this site were checked on November 13, 2026 by loading the official pages. When we show a fee, it comes from the official page text on that date, and we link the source next to it. If we could not confirm a fee from the official page, we say "See the official page for the current fee" instead of guessing.

Some government sites block automated checks. Where that happened, the page says so next to the link.

Fees and rules change

States update fees, forms, and processing times without notice. Always confirm on the official page before you pay or mail anything. If our page and the official page differ, the official page is correct.

Privacy

This is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and has no sign-ups or contact forms. Your web host may keep standard server logs.

Corrections

We re-check pages periodically. Dates on each page show when facts were last checked.
