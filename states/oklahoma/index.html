Frequently asked questions

Where do I get an apostille in Oklahoma?

From the Oklahoma Secretary of State. Federal documents go to the U.S. Department of State instead.

How much is an apostille in Oklahoma?

The official page listed: $25 per apostille ($10 for international adoption proceedings); $20 per authentication (last checked November 15, 2026).

How much does it cost to form an LLC in Oklahoma?

The official source listed: Articles of Organization (Oklahoma LLC): $100 (last checked November 15, 2026).

When is the Oklahoma LLC annual report due?

The official source says: Annual certificate due each year on the LLC's anniversary date ($25) (last checked November 15, 2026).
