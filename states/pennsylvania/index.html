Frequently asked questions

Where do I get an apostille in Pennsylvania?

From the Pennsylvania Department of State. Federal documents go to the U.S. Department of State instead.

How much is an apostille in Pennsylvania?

The official page listed: $15 per document (last checked November 15, 2026).

How much does it cost to form an LLC in Pennsylvania?

The official source listed: Certificate of Organization (domestic LLC): $125 (last checked November 15, 2026).

When is the Pennsylvania LLC annual report due?

The official source says: Due by September 30 each year for LLCs, starting the year after formation (last checked November 15, 2026).
