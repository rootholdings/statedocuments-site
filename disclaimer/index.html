Disclaimer

What this unofficial guide is, how we check facts, and how to use official state offices.

Effective date: November 17, 2026.

This page explains, in more depth, what StateDocuments.com is and is not. It should be read together with our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

This site is unofficial

StateDocuments.com is a private, independent information guide. It is not a government website. It is not run by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any federal, state, or local government, or by any secretary of state or similar office. Official documents, filings, apostilles, and certificates come only from the government offices we link to.

Not legal advice

Nothing on this site is legal, tax, or financial advice, and using it does not create an attorney-client relationship. If you need advice about your situation, consult a licensed professional or the government office itself.

How we source and check facts

For every state, we start from the official government page and link to it first. When we publish a fee, due date, or processing time, it comes from the official page text on the date we checked it. Each page shows a “Last checked” date. If we could not confirm a fact from the official page, we say so — usually “See the official page” or “Check with the state” — instead of guessing. Some government sites block automated checks; where that happened, the page says so next to the link.

Fees, forms, and procedures change. Facts on this site were last checked on November 15, 2026. If our page and the official page differ, the official page is correct. Confirm the current fee and steps with the state before you pay or mail anything.

Official forms are free or cheaper from the state

You can obtain forms and file documents directly with the official state or federal office. Official forms are typically free to download from the government site. Filing and certificate fees are paid to the government. Private companies may offer to do the work for you and charge their own fees on top of the state fee. That is optional. Starting from the official page is usually the least expensive way to file or request a document.

Money from links

We currently earn no money from links on this site. There are no ads and no affiliate links. If that changes, we will update this page with a clear disclosure before any paid or affiliate links go live.
