Browse by document type

Apostilles

A certificate that lets a U.S. public document be accepted in another country. Issued by a state office (usually the Secretary of State) or, for federal documents, the U.S. Department of State.

Open guide

Business filings

Forming an LLC or corporation, filing annual reports, and getting a certificate of good standing all happen at your state's business filing office.

Open guide

Vital records

Certified copies of birth, death, marriage and divorce records come from the state or county vital records office where the event happened.

Open guide

Court records

Case files and certified court copies come from the clerk of the court that handled the case. Each state judiciary explains how to search and request them.

Open guide

UCC and other filings

UCC financing statements (liens on business property) are usually filed with the Secretary of State. We explain what they are and where to look.
