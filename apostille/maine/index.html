Getting an apostille in Maine

In Maine, apostilles for state and local documents are issued by the Maine Secretary of State. Below: the steps, the fee, how long it takes, and where to send your request, in plain English. The official page has the full, current instructions.

Official office

Maine Secretary of State

Open the official apostille page

www.maine.gov

Link checked November 15, 2026.

Fee

$10 per certification.

As of November 15, 2026. Source: official page. Confirm before paying, since fees change.

Where and how to submit

Maine Secretary of State

Mail (appointment for more than 5 documents)

Full addresses, forms and hours: www.maine.gov

Processing time

10–15 business days.

As of November 15, 2026. Source: official page. Times change with volume.

Step by step

  1. Order vital records from Maine DHHS; if ordering by mail, you can ask DHHS to forward them straight to the Secretary of State.
  2. Download and fill out the Request for Authentications/Apostilles form (PDF).
  3. Send the form, the documents and $10 per certification (U.S. funds, payable to "Secretary of State").
  4. For more than 5 documents, call 207-624-7752 for an appointment first.

Steps summarized in our own words from the official page on November 15, 2026. If anything differs, follow the official page.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Check that notarized documents use a complete notarial acknowledgment; the office posts a sample.

What this office can apostille

Generally, documents issued by a Maine public official (such as certified copies of vital records, court records, or state certificates) and documents notarized by a Maine notary. Documents from other states go to that state. Federal documents, such as FBI background checks, go to the U.S. Department of State.

Before you start, ask whoever will receive the document abroad whether they need an apostille or a different certificate (countries outside the Hague Convention use authentication). Getting this wrong is the most common reason people have to start over.

Questions and answers

Who issues apostilles in Maine?

The Maine Secretary of State issues apostilles for documents issued by Maine public officials or notarized by a Maine notary. Documents from other states go to that state, and federal documents go to the U.S. Department of State.

How much does a Maine apostille cost?

The official page listed: $10 per certification (last checked November 15, 2026). Confirm before paying, since fees change.

How long does a Maine apostille take?

The official source listed: 10–15 business days (last checked November 15, 2026). Times change with volume.

How do I submit an apostille request in Maine?

Mail (appointment for more than 5 documents)
