Getting an apostille in West Virginia

In West Virginia, apostilles for state and local documents are issued by the West Virginia Secretary of State. Below: the steps, the fee, how long it takes, and where to send your request, in plain English. The official page has the full, current instructions.

Step by step

  1. Use a document attested by a proper West Virginia officer (notary, county clerk, State Registrar). If a certified copy is available from the filing office, you must use it.
  2. Fill out the Apostille and Certification Request (Form AC-1).
  3. Send the form, documents and fee to the Secretary of State as the official page directs. Expedited service is available.

Steps summarized in our own words from the official page on November 13, 2026. If anything differs, follow the official page.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Deeds, wills and estate papers need certified copies signed by the Clerk of the County Commission; court orders need the issuing Clerk of Court's signature.
  • Read the office's FAQ before submitting to avoid delays.

What this office can apostille

Generally, documents issued by a West Virginia public official (such as certified copies of vital records, court records, or state certificates) and documents notarized by a West Virginia notary. Documents from other states go to that state. Federal documents, such as FBI background checks, go to the U.S. Department of State.

Before you start, ask whoever will receive the document abroad whether they need an apostille or a different certificate (countries outside the Hague Convention use authentication). Getting this wrong is the most common reason people have to start over.

Questions and answers

Who issues apostilles in West Virginia?

The West Virginia Secretary of State issues apostilles for documents issued by West Virginia public officials or notarized by a West Virginia notary. Documents from other states go to that state, and federal documents go to the U.S. Department of State.

How much does a West Virginia apostille cost?

The official page listed: $10 per certification; additional documents signed by the same official are $5 each (checked November 13, 2026). Confirm before paying, since fees change.

How long does a West Virginia apostille take?

We could not confirm a processing time on the official page on November 13, 2026. Check the official page or contact the office.

How do I submit an apostille request in West Virginia?

Mail or in person

Do I need an apostille or an authentication?

It depends on the destination country. Countries in the Hague Apostille Convention accept apostilles; other countries usually need an authentication followed by further steps. Ask whoever will receive the document which one they want.
