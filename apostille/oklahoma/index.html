Getting an apostille in Oklahoma

In Oklahoma, apostilles for state and local documents are issued by the Oklahoma Secretary of State. This page summarizes what we could confirm; the official page has the full, current instructions.

Official office

Oklahoma Secretary of State

Open the official apostille page

oklahoma.gov

Link checked November 12, 2026.

Fee

$25 per apostille ($10 for international adoption proceedings); $20 per authentication.

As of November 12, 2026. Source: official page. Confirm before paying, since fees change.

What this office can apostille

Generally, documents issued by a Oklahoma public official (such as certified copies of vital records, court records, or state certificates) and documents notarized by a Oklahoma notary. Documents from other states go to that state. Federal documents go to the U.S. Department of State.

Notes we confirmed on the official page

  • We did not record state-specific notes. Read the official page for requirements.

Before you send anything

  1. Confirm the destination country and whether it uses apostilles.
  2. Get a recent certified copy from the office that holds the record. Photocopies are generally refused.
  3. Follow the official page for the request form, payment method, and return shipping.
