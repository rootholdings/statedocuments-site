Privacy Policy

How StateDocuments.com handles information today. This page describes only what is true as of the effective date.

Effective date: November 17, 2026.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information from you on this site. There are no accounts, sign-ups, comment boxes, or contact forms. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, address, or payment details.

Some pages include a drop-down or a search box that helps you jump to a state or filter a list. Those tools run only in your browser. They do not send what you type to us, and they do not store it.

Hosting logs

This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run our own server logs. GitHub's documentation states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. We do not receive those logs. For details, see GitHub's explanation of Pages data collection and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies. It does not use local storage or session storage. We do not use analytics, advertising, or social-media scripts. We do not load fonts, style sheets, or scripts from third-party networks.

Links to state sites and other websites

We link to official state and federal websites and to other outside sites so you can reach the office that handles your document. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Once you follow a link off this site, their policies apply. We are not responsible for their content or how they handle information.

Children

This site is a general information guide. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we have no forms or accounts, we have no way to collect that information on this site.

U.S. state privacy rights

Some U.S. states give residents rights to ask what personal information a business holds about them, to access it, or to ask that it be deleted or corrected. Because we do not collect personal information from you on this site, we typically would not have information of that kind to provide or delete. GitHub may hold server logs as described in its own privacy statement. A contact email will be added to this page soon.

If this changes

If we add email signups, analytics, advertising, affiliate links, or any other feature that collects or shares information, we will update this page before that change goes live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page will change when we do. The updated policy will apply to your use of the site after it is posted.
