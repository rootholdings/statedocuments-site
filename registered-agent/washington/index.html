Registered agent requirements in Washington

What official Washington sources say about who may serve as a registered agent, address rules, changing an agent, and how an agent resigns. This is an unofficial guide; check with the state before you file.

Official office

Washington Secretary of State, business filings

Who may serve as a registered agent

Washington law creates two types of registered agent: a Non-Commercial Registered Agent and a Commercial Registered Agent. A Non-Commercial Registered Agent is any person or business with an address in Washington who agrees to accept legal mail on behalf of the entity. A person or business may be listed as a Commercial Registered Agent by filing a Commercial Listing Statement.

Physical address requirements

The registered agent must maintain a physical address in Washington State. PO Boxes or PMBs are prohibited as that physical address.

Fee to change a registered agent

The official Non-Commercial Registered Agent Statement of Change form says there is no filing fee to update or change the registered agent.

Form or process to change a registered agent

Check with the state. Official source: https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities

How a registered agent resigns

To resign, file a Statement of Resignation with the Secretary of State. Resignations take effect 31 days after filing is placed on record.
