Registered agent requirements in Wisconsin

What official Wisconsin sources say about who may serve as a registered agent, address rules, changing an agent, and how an agent resigns. This is an unofficial guide; check with the state before you file.

Official office

Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions

Open the official business filing site

dfi.wi.gov

Who may serve as a registered agent

The entity may not name itself as its own registered agent.

Physical address requirements

The registered office address must be an actual physical location with a street address, and not solely a P.O. Box, mailbox service, or telephone answering service. The registered office address must be identical to the registered agent's business office.

Fee to change a registered agent

$25.00 to file a Statement of Change of Registered Agent/Office, or $10.00 if filed online (Statement of Change Online Filing). The same amounts appear across the entity-type columns on the official fee table.

Form or process to change a registered agent

Form 13, Statement of Change.

How a registered agent resigns

$10.00 to file a Resignation of Registered Agent. The same amount appears across the entity-type columns on the official fee table.
